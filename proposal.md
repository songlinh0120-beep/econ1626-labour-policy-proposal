# Recommendations and specific practices for the Australian labour market to address the AI revolution
###To: Australian Department of Employment and Workplace Relations  
###Policy focus: Labour market adjustment, skills policy, and responsible workplace AI adoption

## Executive Summary
AI will not only replace people's jobs but also reassign tasks to workers and businesses. Therefore, the market risk facing Australia is not mass unemployment, but uneven distribution of tasks, wage pressures on day-to-day jobs, and uneven access to AI-driven productivity gains. Based on economic evidence, labor demand declines when automation replaces labor tasks, but employment can recover when new tasks are created and workers are able to transfer to them. Therefore, the Australian government should adopt targeted AI task transformation strategies, such as identifying tasks exposed to AI, funding practical retraining, and supporting businesses to use AI to enhance productivity rather than simply replace labor. It not only makes good use of AI to improve productivity, but also protects employees who may be affected by AI and uneven distribution of tasks.
